Privacy Policy

FiveFour — last updated August 2026

What we collect

When you use the contact form on this website, we receive the name, email address and message you choose to send. The form is processed by Web3Forms, a third-party form service, which stores the submission so we can read it. If you write to us on WhatsApp, the messages are covered by WhatsApp's own privacy terms.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and talk about your project.
  • To send you a quote or proposal you asked for.
  • To keep a record of our conversation while a project is active.

We do not sell your details, share them with advertisers, or add you to marketing lists.

Cookies and analytics

This site does not set tracking cookies. If we later add analytics (for example Google Analytics on the sites we build for clients), that is set up on the client's own site and described there.

How long we keep it

Enquiry messages are kept until the project is finished or you ask us to delete them — whichever comes first.

Your rights

You can ask us what we hold about you, correct it, or delete it at any time. Email fivefour.codes@gmail.com and we'll sort it out.
