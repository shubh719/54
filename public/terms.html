Terms of Service

FiveFour — last updated August 2026

What you get

We design and build the website described in your plan and scope discussion. Prices are one-time and cover the work in that scope. Things not listed in the scope — new pages, new features, extra revisions — are quoted separately before we start them.

What we need from you

  • Your text, photos and contact details, so the site can be built and launched.
  • A clear idea of what your business does, if you have one — we'll help you shape it.
  • Access to your domain registrar or hosting account when it's time to go live.

Payment

We agree on the price before work starts. A deposit is required to book your slot; the balance is due before launch. Details are confirmed in writing for every project.

Revisions

We include a fair number of revision rounds in every plan so the site ends up right. Structural changes — a different layout, a rewritten page purpose — count as new work and are quoted separately.

Domains and hosting

Domain and hosting are billed separately at actual cost, paid by you directly to the registrar or host, or through us at the same price. You own your domain and content. We keep copies for backup and maintenance while the project is active.

Support after launch

Support covers bug fixes on what we built, for the period stated in your plan. It does not cover new pages or features — those are quoted as paid add-ons.

Our side of the bargain

We deliver on time, keep you informed, and use plain language. If we make a mistake, we fix it. If a project is cancelled after work has started, we invoice fairly for the work already done.
